Our Club

The Baruch Esports Association was founded by League of Legends fanatics. Eventually we started branching into other major titles, like Starcraft and CSGO.

In the current day, we operate both competitively and casually. We host a space on campus for casual gamers, with bi-weekly events.

We also operate competitively, having teams in VALORANT, League of Legends, Overwatch 2, iRacing, Mario Kart and more.

Our club welcomes gamers of any level!
